# Brazilian E-Commerce Performance Analytics
**SQL Server | Python (Pandas) | 100K+ Orders | 2016–2018**
---
## About This Project
I analyzed 100,000+ real orders from Olist, Brazil's largest e-commerce platform.
The goal was to answer the kind of questions a real business team would ask —
across Marketing, Finance, Product, Sales, and Customer Support.
---
## What I Found
- **96,478** delivered orders analyzed
- Customer satisfaction was **59.1%** in 2018 — dropped to 50.99% in March
- **Black Friday 2017** permanently doubled monthly order volumes
- **Credit cards** generated the most revenue on the platform
- Repurchase rate was under **1.5%** — most customers never came back
- **São Paulo** had the highest revenue and fastest delivery across all 27 states
---
## What I Analyzed
| Area | What I looked at |
|---|---|
| Customer Satisfaction | Monthly 5-star rates, where satisfaction dropped and why |
| Order Trends | Year over year growth and Black Friday impact |
| Payments | Which payment methods made the most money |
| Product Revenue | Top categories by orders and revenue |
| Delivery Performance | Average delivery days by state |
| Seller Performance | Top sellers by revenue and order volume |
| Repurchase Behavior | Which categories had the lowest repeat buyers |
| RFM Segmentation | Grouped customers into Diamond, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Churned |
| Cohort Retention | Tracked how many customers came back month by month |
